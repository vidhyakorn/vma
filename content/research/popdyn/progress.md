### Pneumococcal Disease Burden — Thailand

{{< milestone date="2025 Q1" status="done" >}}
**Project initiated.** Thai Research Fund (TRF) grant confirmed. Scope agreed: age-stratified burden-of-disease model for invasive pneumococcal disease (IPD) and non-bacteraemic pneumococcal pneumonia (NBP) in Thailand, feeding into a cost-effectiveness analysis of PCV valency switching.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Now" status="now" >}}
**Data assembly and model structure.** Building the epidemiological compartmental model skeleton; linking to national surveillance datasets (MOPH, Bureau of Epidemiology) and published carriage studies for parameter estimation.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Next" status="next" >}}
**Calibration and validation.** Bayesian calibration of serotype-specific transmission parameters against pre-vaccine and post-PCV10/13 surveillance data.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Later" status="next" >}}
**Health economic layer.** Cost-effectiveness analysis comparing PCV13, PCV15, PCV20 strategies at current Thai vaccine schedule and alternative schedules.
{{< /milestone >}}

### AHR-SEA: Helminths Transmission Model

{{< milestone date="2025 Q2" status="done" >}}
**Consortium onboarding.** Joined the Asia Helminth Research (AHR-SEA) consortium. Role defined: build a region-adapted transmission model for soil-transmitted helminths (STH) capturing heterogeneous exposure, MDA coverage, and re-infection dynamics.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Now" status="now" >}}
**Model design and literature synthesis.** Reviewing published helminth transmission models (Anderson–May family) and adapting to SEA school-age population structure, sanitation coverage data, and MDA programme records.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Next" status="next" >}}
**Parameterisation from systematic review.** Evidence synthesis (EvidSyn arm) will feed intensity-of-infection and egg-output estimates directly into the transmission model.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Later" status="next" >}}
**Scenario analysis.** Simulating impact of alternative MDA strategies (frequency, coverage, age-targeting) and WASH interventions on worm burden and DALYs averted.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="2024" status="done" >}}
**Model developed and submitted.** Deterministic SEIR compartmental model of cross-border COVID-19 transmission at the Thailand–Myanmar border, incorporating population movement data and heterogeneous vaccination coverage.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="2025" status="done" >}}
**Published in BMC Public Health.** Full paper including scenario analysis of border control policies and vaccination thresholds required for epidemic suppression.
