#### **Demographic, Epidemiological, and Economic Modelling of Real Populations**

---

Population dynamics research sits at the intersection of infectious disease epidemiology, health economics, and demographic modelling. The goal is to translate biological and behavioural mechanisms — transmission, immunity, migration, age-structure — into quantitative decision tools that health systems and policymakers can actually use.

Models here range from deterministic compartmental ODE systems to individual-based simulation, calibrated against local surveillance data and validated against independent outcomes. Health economic evaluation (cost-effectiveness, burden-of-disease) runs alongside each modelling effort.
