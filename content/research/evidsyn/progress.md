### STH Systematic Review + NMA — AHR-SEA

{{< milestone date="2026 Q1" status="done" >}}
**Protocol registered and PROSPERO submission.** PICOS defined: MDA interventions (albendazole, mebendazole, ivermectin, combination) in school-age children and preschool-age children across SEA countries; outcomes: egg-reduction rate (ERR), cure rate (CR), intensity categories. Search strategy agreed with AHR-SEA data team.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="2026 Q2–Q3" status="done" >}}
**Database search and title-abstract screening.** PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, WHO IRIS, grey literature. Duplicates removed; title/abstract screening completed by two independent reviewers with third-party arbitration.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Now" status="now" >}}
**Full-text review and data extraction.** Reading full texts of included records; extracting outcome data, programme context, coverage levels, and equity indicators (urban/rural, SES, ethnicity). Risk of bias assessed with RoB 2 / ROBINS-I.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Next" status="next" >}}
**Network meta-analysis.** Bayesian NMA in R (gemtc / BUGSnet) comparing MDA regimens head-to-head. Subgroup analyses by helminth species (Ascaris, Trichuris, hookworm) and age group. Inconsistency checks and node-splitting.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Later" status="next" >}}
**Manuscript and transmission model linkage.** Write-up of systematic review and NMA findings; pass intensity-of-infection and treatment-efficacy estimates to PopDyn helminth transmission model for integrated scenario analysis.
{{< /milestone >}}
