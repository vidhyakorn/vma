#### **Systematic Reviews, Meta-Analysis, and Evidence Integration Methods**

---

Evidence synthesis work develops and applies formal methods for combining evidence across studies — from traditional pairwise meta-analysis through to network meta-analysis (NMA), indirect comparisons, and methods for handling complex heterogeneity including ecological settings, programme contexts, and mixed intervention platforms.

A secondary thread concerns methodology itself: how bias enters synthesis, what structural assumptions NMA requires, and how to represent uncertainty in ways that are honest to policymakers.
