### CARP Framework

{{< milestone date="2024" status="done" >}}
**Conceptual architecture drafted.** Core CARP framework document written: mapping Sen's capability vectors to health-economic instruments, identifying the gap between ICECAP-type instruments and the bio-psycho-social complexity of neurodevelopmental and chronic conditions.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="2025" status="done" >}}
**CALY-SWE and ICECAP reviewed.** Deep review of existing capabilities-based health economic instruments — CALY-SWE (Sweden), ICECAP-A, ICECAP-O, ICECAP-SCM — to identify item coverage, psychometric performance, and transferability to Thai settings.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Now" status="now" >}}
**Theoretical grounding and formal specification.** Writing the normative paper that formally specifies CARP: what capability domains are included, how functionings map to freedom sets, and how capability-adjusted outcomes can be compared across interventions.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Next" status="next" >}}
**Instrument design for Thai population.** Designing BWS and TTO instruments to elicit capability weights from a Thai general population sample and from subgroups of persons with NDCs — in collaboration with NDLS WP4/WP5.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Later" status="next" >}}
**Empirical weight study.** Field data collection. Statistical analysis (latent class, mixed logit) to produce population-level and subgroup capability weights for use in CALY computation.
{{< /milestone >}}

{{< milestone date="Later" status="next" >}}
**Integration across frameworks.** Feeding CARP capability indices into NDLS (agent-based simulation) and PopDyn (health economic layer) as the shared evaluation metric — so that all three domain frameworks report outcomes on the same scale.
{{< /milestone >}}
