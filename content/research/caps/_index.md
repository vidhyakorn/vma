#### **Empirical and Normative Capabilities Approach Research for Health Policy**

---

The Capabilities Approach (CA) — developed by Amartya Sen and Martha Nussbaum — evaluates human well-being not by income or utility but by what people are genuinely *able to do and be*. Applied to health economics and policy, this means asking: does this intervention expand the real freedoms people have to live flourishing lives?

CApS develops the theoretical, empirical, and computational infrastructure for capabilities-based evaluation in the Thai and Southeast Asian context: preference elicitation instruments, capability weighting studies, and the CARP framework that bridges CA theory to quantitative policy analysis.
